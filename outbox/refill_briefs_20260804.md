## Brief 1 — singing_dunes — LONG (9-11 cards)
**Concept:** A dune sea at deep dusk under the shredded rings of a broken moon —
wind-carved ripple trains crossing at angles down every slip-face — dives to one
crest, into a heap of sand grains where every grain is a different jewel mineral,
and inside one translucent grain to crossed crystal-plane fringes.
**Palette family:** mirage (burnt sienna / rose-gold / deep violet dusk).
**Math personality:** moiré fringes — two overlapping wave trains everywhere at
every scale: crossing dune ripple sets, interfering grain-lattice planes. First
use in the catalog.
**Space personality:** tidally-shredded moon rings raking a violet sky — ring
shards combed into density-wave grooves. First use.
**Seam idea:** pattern ↔ space, same physics at two scales — the dunes' ripple
wave-trains ARE the ring system's density-wave grooves (a ring is a dune sea in
orbit); the fold rides that rhyme out and the return falls from the rings back
onto the dune crests.
**Tier:** LONG, 9-11 cards.

## Brief 2 — moon_jelly — LONG (9-11 cards)
**Concept:** A midnight lagoon of moon jellyfish — a fleet of translucent bells
pulsing under still black water — dives through one bell into its trailing
tentacle streamers, whose braided strands wind into knot-canyons, and folds out
where a star-cradle fires its own glowing twin jets.
**Palette family:** abyssal opal (translucent pearl / pale rose / ice blue
glowing on navy-black).
**Math personality:** knot-theory braids — tentacle strands as woven braid
canyons, crossing over and under with real braid logic. First use.
**Space personality:** Herbig-Haro jets — a cocooned newborn star driving two
luminous polar jets through dark dust. First use; no nebula, no galaxy.
**Seam idea:** creature ↔ space — a pulsing bell trailing streamers and a jetted
star-cocoon share one silhouette; the braid realm folds out into the twin-jet
cradle, and the return sinks from the jets' glow back into the lagoon's bells.
**Tier:** LONG, 9-11 cards.

## Brief 3 — vinyl_canyon — SHORT (4-5 cards)
**Concept:** A spinning vinyl record under one amber lamp, seen from directly
above as a dark disc of glinting concentric arcs; the dive drops past the stylus
into a groove canyon whose walls are the engraved sound-wave itself, down to the
pressed-vinyl pebble texture of the canyon floor. Never leaves the turntable.
**Palette family:** petrol sheen (oil-slick green-violet shimmer on charcoal
black, one warm amber lamp glint).
**Math personality:** the Archimedean spiral + engraved waveform ridge-trains —
music made visible as terrain; distinct from the catalog's logarithmic nacre
spiral and its Chladni sand figures.
**Space personality:** none — realm-local; the glinting groove arcs play the
sky.
**Seam idea:** pattern → the same pattern writ large — the micro ridge-field on
the canyon floor folds back out into the whole disc's spiral of arcs seen from
above. Realm-local loop, no cosmos.
**Tier:** SHORT, 4-5 cards.

## Brief 4 — lantern_canals — LONG (9-11 cards)
**Concept:** An empty ring-canal city at night — concentric lamplit canals
nested like orbits around a harbor lighthouse, bridges braiding between the
rings — dives from the ringed city map down onto a quay, into the lighthouse's
concentric Fresnel lens, and out where the ring pattern hangs in the sky as a
perfect circle of starfire.
**Palette family:** cobalt & whitewash (cobalt night / lime-white walls / brass
lamplight).
**Math personality:** KAM orbit tori — nested rings with braided island chains
between them, the city map as an orbit diagram. First use.
**Space personality:** a ring galaxy — a closed hoop of starfire around a
golden core; first use, and the catalog's only circular galaxy.
**Seam idea:** circle-lattice ↔ ring galaxy — the Fresnel lens's concentric
glass ridges fold out into the galaxy's hoop; the return falls through the hoop
to the canal rings glowing below and lands back on the water.
**Tier:** LONG, 9-11 cards.
