# Refill briefs — 2026-08-02 (coordinator: Fable)

Five briefs, maximally distinct from the active catalog and from each other.
Each composer follows .claude/skills/journey-composer/SKILL.md exactly; the brief
only seeds it. Palette families, math families, and seam types are all
first-uses or near-first-uses in the catalog.

---

## Brief 1 — heron_delta — SHORT (4-5 cards)
**Concept:** A silver tidal estuary at dawn, seen first as braided channels from
the air, narrows to a lone grey heron preening — the dive goes into one feather,
down the barb ridges to the interlocking barbule hooks, where the crossed
translucent combs make drifting moiré fringes.
**Palette family:** arctic (ice blues / white / silver).
**Math personality:** moiré fringes between two drifting grids (the overlapping
barbule combs).
**Seam idea:** texture→landscape — the barbule zip-ridges read as the estuary's
braided tidal channels writ small; the seam folds the moiré lattice back out
into the aerial braided delta the journey opened on. No cosmos.
**Tier:** SHORT, 4-5 cards.

## Brief 2 — gossamer_dawn — SHORT (4-5 cards)
**Concept:** A hedgerow at first light strung with dozens of dew-beaded orb
webs; the dive rides one silk radial inward along the web's logarithmic spiral
to a single dew bead, then enters the bead's lensed interior where the whole
meadow hangs inverted in miniature.
**Palette family:** pastel dawn (peach / lavender / mint).
**Math personality:** curves — the orb web's radial-plus-logarithmic-spiral
geometry as the structural motif.
**Seam idea:** image-carrier jump — the miniature meadow lensed inside the dew
bead becomes the real dawn meadow/hedgerow outside, closing the loop without
leaving the hedge. No cosmos.
**Tier:** SHORT, 4-5 cards.

## Brief 3 — lichen_moor — MEDIUM (6-8 cards)
**Concept:** A dusk moorland dotted with standing stones; one stone's face is a
planet seen from orbit, its lichen patches reading as continents. The dive goes
through the lichen's thallus forests into the stone's percolation crack-lattice.
**Palette family:** jade & vermilion (celadon / sage green + rust-orange lichen).
**Math personality:** percolation crack-lattices (the weathered stone's fracture
network).
**Seam idea:** pattern → the same pattern writ large in the origin scene — the
crack lattice folds out into the network of dry-stone walls veining the moor
seen from above, then settles back to the stone circle. Terrestrial throughout;
the stone-as-planet plays the "space" role.
**Tier:** MEDIUM, 6-8 cards.

## Brief 4 — night_lido — SHORT (4-5 cards)
**Concept:** A night-lit open-air swimming pool, lamps below the waterline: the
caustic light-web dancing on the pool floor is the whole world. The dive drops
into one bright caustic node and travels the filament web as it sharpens and
rebranches with depth.
**Palette family:** jewel tones (sapphire water / emerald caustics on black).
**Math personality:** caustic light-webs (pool-bottom shimmer as living
geometry).
**Seam idea:** glowing-things-as-sky — the deepest caustic lattice folds up into
the field of moonlit ripple-glints on the pool's surface, a "starfield" that is
still the same pool, landing back at the tiled poolside. No cosmos.
**Tier:** SHORT, 4-5 cards.

## Brief 5 — cinder_veil — LONG (9-11 cards)
**Concept:** A volcanic caldera country at night — glowing vent-veins across
black land — dives down a cooling lava river to basalt terraces and into a
single pumice stone, whose nested gas bubbles become an Apollonian gasket foam;
the exotic seam blows that foam out into a supernova-remnant lace veil, and the
return descends past a rogue dark planet veined with fire back into ash clouds
over the caldera.
**Palette family:** ember & ash (orange fire on charcoal).
**Math personality:** Apollonian gasket foam (pumice vesicles, circles nested in
circles).
**Space personality:** supernova-remnant lace veil + a rogue dark planet with
fire veins — no nebula, no spiral galaxy.
**Seam idea:** exotic micro↔cosmos — the Apollonian bubble foam and the
supernova lace share filament-lace structure; more frames, same intensity.
**Tier:** LONG, 9-11 cards.
