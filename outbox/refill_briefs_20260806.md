## Brief 2 — ochre_door — SHORT (4-5 cards)
**Concept:** A sun-bleached olive-green door in an ochre adobe wall at hard noon
— the dive drops into the peeling paint's chip archipelago, along the flaking
edges (each chip boundary a fractal coastline) into the exposed strata cliffs of
decades of repaint, down to the pigment platelet terraces — which fold out into
the banded painted-desert mesa country above the town, and home to the door in
its wall. Never a harbor, never dusk: the catalog's first hard-noon journey.
**Palette family:** olive & terracotta (sun-bleached olive paint / ochre dust /
terracotta clay on burnt-umber shadow) — new family added tonight; noon

**Math personality:** Mandelbrot/Julia tendril coastlines — every chipped paint
edge is a self-similar tendril coastline, the same filigree at every depth;
first use.
**Space personality:** none — realm-local; the mesa sky is empty noon glare.
**Seam idea:** texture → landscape — the pigment strata's banded terraces ARE
the painted-desert mesa bands: the wrap rides one ochre band out until it is a
mesa wall with the adobe town tiny below. First paint-to-geology carrier.
**Tier:** SHORT, 4-5 cards.

## Brief 3 — comet_firn — LONG (9-11 cards)
**Concept:** A fjord under the midnight sun — over the water to the glacier
front, into a crevasse's blue chasm, to the ancient trapped air-bubble

Glacier ice and comet ice are the same substance at two scales.
**Palette family:** midnight sun (low honey-gold light / teal glacier ice / long
violet shadows) — new family added tonight; warm-gold-on-teal, unlike arctic's
silver-blue and abyssal opal's rose-pearl.
**Math personality:** laminar streamline foliation — flow-lines combed around
obstacles: the glacier's banded foliation, bubbles strung along flow paths,
comet-tail streams; new family added tonight, first use.
**Space personality:** the Oort comet halo + one active comet's streaming coma —
no nebula, no galaxy, no star close-up; the last big unused personality.
**Seam idea:** same swarm at two scales, cosmic fold — the trapped air-bubble
constellations in blue ice ARE the Oort halo's comet swarm (round pale bodies
hanging in a dark medium, strung along streamlines); the return closes
texture-wise too (comet ice cliffs → glacier front).
**Tier:** LONG, 9-11 cards.
