## Brief 1 — salt_mirror — MEDIUM (6-8 cards)
**Concept:** Salt evaporation ponds at dusk — an aerial patchwork of rose, copper
and jade mirror-pools doubled by a binary sunset reflected in every pane — dives
onto the white crust shore and into stepped halite hopper crystals, right-angled
cube terraces descending into a pure cubic lattice.
**Palette family:** pastel dawn (peach / lavender / mint, rose-salt pinks).
**Math personality:** cubic crystal lattices — stepped HOPPER cubes, strict right
angles and staircase recesses; never generic sparkle-lattice (sugar/ice lattices
already exist in the catalog — this one is architectural, Escher-stepped).
**Space personality:** none as a stretch — the binary sunset (two suns, doubled
again by reflection) lives in the establishing and return scenes.
**Seam idea:** pattern → the same pattern writ large in the origin scene — the
cubic lattice's glowing grid folds back out into the rectangular grid of sunset
ponds seen from the air. No cosmos.
**Tier:** MEDIUM, 6-8 cards.

## Brief 2 — attic_drift — SHORT (4-5 cards)
**Concept:** A dark attic split by one wide amber sunbeam alive with drifting
dust motes; the dive enters the beam and the mote swarm becomes a slow galaxy —
down to a single translucent mote, a jagged glassy boulder turning in the light.
**Palette family:** monochrome + ONE accent (deep umber dark + a single amber).
**Math personality:** none heavy — the identity is the mote swarm itself, depth
on depth of drifting points; keep every card a field of many floating things.
**Seam idea:** glowing-things-as-sky — the mote swarm IS the cosmos; from the
mote's surface the surrounding lit swarm reads as a starfield that resolves back
into the attic sunbeam. Realm-local loop, never leaves the attic.
**Tier:** SHORT, 4-5 cards.

## Brief 3 — jewel_oculus — LONG (9-11 cards)
**Concept:** A vast empty jewel-glass palace at dusk — girih star-lattice dome,
stained light pooled on stone — dives into a single glass star-tile, through the
ten-fold girih strapwork into an E8 quasicrystal starburst; the exotic seam blows
the starburst out into a stained-glass cosmic web, and the return passes lensed
Einstein-ring arcs and a lensed low sun before dawn light through the dome's
oculus lands back in the hall.
**Palette family:** jewel tones (ruby / sapphire / emerald on black).
**Math personality:** girih star-lattices → E8/quasicrystal starburst projection
(one coherent family: girih IS a quasicrystal slice; ten-fold stars throughout —
distinct from the catalog's Penrose kites and hyperbolic rim).
**Space personality:** stained-glass nebula + gravitational-lens Einstein rings
and arcs — the cosmos rendered in the journey's own leaded-glass material; no
spiral galaxy, no default nebula.
**Seam idea:** exotic micro↔cosmos — E8 starburst → stained-glass cosmic web;
shared radiating-strut structure carries the jump.
**Tier:** LONG, 9-11 cards.

## Brief 4 — turing_springs — MEDIUM (6-8 cards)
**Concept:** Rainbow hot-spring terraces seen from the air — dozens of steaming
mineral pools ringed fire-orange around deep teal eyes — dive to one pool rim
where the microbial mat breaks into reaction-diffusion Turing stripes and spots,
down into the thermophile filament forest inside one stripe.
**Palette family:** ember & ash (fire-orange rims on charcoal basalt, one deep
teal counter-note in the pool eyes).
**Math personality:** reaction-diffusion Turing stripes/spots (animal-coat math
grown by microbes) — first use in the catalog.
**Space personality:** a banded gas giant with towering aurora curtains — bands
and curtains, not storms (the catalog's gas giant is a hexagonal pole storm).
**Seam idea:** texture → cosmic object — the microbial Turing bands and the gas
giant's latitude bands are the same stripe-math at two scales; the return falls
from the aurora curtains into steam clouds over the terraces.
**Tier:** MEDIUM, 6-8 cards.

## Brief 5 — droplet_zoo — SHORT (4-5 cards)
**Concept:** A moonlit pond dip: one water drop sitting on a glass slide holds a
teeming plankton zoo; the dive enters the drop, crosses the swarm to a diatom's
geodesic glass shell and into its silica strut lattice.
**Palette family:** iridescent pearl (oil-slick rainbow shimmer on cream glass).
**Math personality:** geodesic domes / radiolarian strut lattices — the diatom
shell as glass architecture; first use in the catalog.
**Seam idea:** image-carrier jump — deep in the silica lattice the struts frame
a lensed inverted image of the moonlit pond (the droplet is the lens); the dive
passes through it back out to the night pond and the slide. Realm-local, no
cosmos.
**Tier:** SHORT, 4-5 cards.
