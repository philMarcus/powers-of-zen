## Brief 1 — `selenite_hothouse` · LONG (9-11 cards) · format.frames_per_beat = 7 (assigned)
**Concept:** the Cave of Crystals — Naica, Mexico: a mine chamber 300 m down where
selenite (gypsum) beams eleven metres long cross one another in every direction in air
hot and wet as a hothouse; sealed inside the crystals are pockets of ancient brine with
bacteria still dormant after fifty thousand years. A MINERAL dive that carries a living
cellular card: from a great void's ghost web (cosmic-web one-off, new) down a dwarf
galaxy to a Wolf–Rayet wind bubble (stellar one-off, new) and the blue star burning
off-centre in it, to a desert world, the sierra by night, the mine-yard commons (the
populated card — kit fox, kangaroo rats, a great horned owl on the gantry, hawk-moths at
the yucca, free-tailed bats streaming from the adit; the headframe and hoist are made
things, empty, no one present), down the shaft into the crossed-beam chamber, onto one
beam's glassy face with its sealed pockets, INTO a brine pocket (the cellular card:
brine-pocket sleepers, REALMS 09-06 — a lens of brine, one vapour bead, dormant rod and
sphere cells adrift). The pocket is the small end; the journey's emblem is "a sealed
bubble with something alive in it" restated at every scale (the void with its ghost web,
the wind bubble with its star, the pocket with its sleepers). Reach exp ≥ 22; the small
end stops at the cellular band ON PURPOSE (no atom, no nucleus — this is the one long
tonight that does not run the atom ladder).
**Palette family:** selenite glass & indigo steam (VARIATIONS 2026-09-06).
**Math/space personality:** jackstraws (VARIATIONS 2026-09-06) — random crossed rods: the
beams in the chamber, the ghost web's crossed tendrils; space personality = the Wolf–Rayet
bubble with its off-centre blower.
**Seam idea:** the brine pocket (a sealed lens with one bead and a few sleeping cells) ↔
the void's ghost web (a sealed hollow of blackness with a faint miniature web of dwarf
galaxies threading it) — a bubble with a little life inside, at two scales. NOTE the seam
card is then the LAST card of the list with the loop-pair being (dwarf galaxy/bubble
side) — arrange the list so the seam is mid-list and the first/last cards are natural
adjacent scales, and remember the render_start rotation rule (i ≠ s, i ≠ s+1).
**Style suggestion:** aurora_silk (crystalline is banned tonight; the cave's steam and the
void's soft web both want silk).
**Music lane suggestion:** choir_of_dust ("hushed, suspended, dissolving" — cells asleep
for fifty thousand years).
