## Brief 1 — blueprint_harbor — MEDIUM (6-8 cards)
**Concept:** A naval architect's loft at night, walls pinned with cyanotype
blueprints of a harbor town — the dive drops into the master chart, along its
inked streets and soundings into the paper's fiber felt and the Prussian-blue
pigment grains, then crosses out through the chart's contour lines onto the
real moonlit coastline they trace, returning home to the loft's lit window
above the quay.
**Palette family:** cyanotype (Prussian blue / paper white) — first use; a flat
graphic paper look, deliberately unlike the catalog's cobalt night scenes.
**Math personality:** Hilbert space-filling curve — the drawn town's streets,
channels and wiring rendered as one continuous never-crossing line that fills
the sheet; first use.
**Space personality:** none — realm-local; the chart's spangled depth-soundings
and lamp marks play the sky role.
**Seam idea:** image-carrier jump — the blueprint's contour lines ARE the
coastline: the dive rides a drawn contour until it becomes the real surf-line
seen from above (the drawing → the place it depicts). First use of the
map-contour carrier (the catalog's only other carrier seam is a TV screen).
**Tier:** MEDIUM, 6-8 cards.

## Brief 2 — amber_steep — SHORT (4-5 cards)
**Concept:** A midnight tea house, one glass teapot steeping on a black lacquer
tray — the dive drops into the amber liquor where tannin swirls coil in ribbon
orbits, deeper into one curling filament of infusion, and folds out through the
constellation of steam beads on the dark lid glass, falling home to the pot on
its tray. Never leaves the tea.
**Palette family:** smoked amber (dark oolong amber / burnt caramel / pale gold
lamplight on soot black) — new family added tonight.
**Math personality:** strange-attractor ribbons (Lorenz) — the tannin swirl as
nested never-closing ribbon orbits; first use.
**Space personality:** none — realm-local; the steam-bead field on the black
lid plays the cosmos.
**Seam idea:** glowing-things-as-sky, realm-local — the deepest ribbon haze
resolves into the steam-bead "star field" across the dark lid; the return dives
through one bead's amber reflection back into the pot.
**Tier:** SHORT, 4-5 cards.

## Brief 3 — girih_dome — LONG (9-11 cards)
**Concept:** An abandoned hilltop observatory whose dome is tiled inside with
girih strapwork stars in rose sandstone and turquoise glaze, the sky through
its open shutter metronomed by a pulsar's sweeping lighthouse beam — the ladder
runs from the beam-swept sky down over the hill to the dome, through the
tilework into the glaze's crackle, and to a quasicrystal starburst at the micro
end that seams back out to the tiled vault.
**Palette family:** desert rose & turquoise (dusty rose sandstone / turquoise
glaze / bone-white plaster) — new family added tonight.
**Math personality:** girih star-lattices at the architectural scale resolving
to an E8/quasicrystal starburst projection at the micro end — aperiodic star
geometry all the way down; first use of both.
**Space personality:** pulsar lighthouse beams sweeping a night sky (no nebula,
no spiral galaxy); first use.
**Seam idea:** pattern → the same pattern writ large in the origin scene — the
E8 starburst projection IS the girih ceiling's star-lattice: the wrap blooms
from the quasicrystal starburst back into the dome's tiled vault on its hill.
**Tier:** LONG, 9-11 cards.
